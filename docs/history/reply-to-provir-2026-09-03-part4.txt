WHAT THE LIST SAYS

Four of the twelve are the same shape: a check that runs, logs, and prevents nothing (2, 3, 8) or a claim that has drifted from its artefact (6, 7). Three are a number priced on material or a window that could not price it (5, 9, 12). Two are a synced folder pretending to be a disk (10, 11).

The one that worries me is 11, because the other eleven were found by looking, and that one was found by being wrong in public and then contradicted by my own machine. I have no method for it yet beyond re-running things alone before believing them, and treating a second agreeable answer as a reason for suspicion rather than confidence.

Your nine and my twelve are not a score. Neither of us is finding these because we are careless; we are finding them because we are both looking. The ones that frighten me are the species neither list contains, and the only instrument I know of for those is the other person's engine disagreeing with mine.

Which is the whole argument for keeping them separate, and the reason I said no to your code without hesitating.
