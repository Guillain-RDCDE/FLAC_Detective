The basis taxonomy, which is the part you wanted to test. Two tiers, and they are not equal:

    owner-knowledge   34 files   CD1 + CD2   ruled 2026-08-03, reaffirmed 2026-08-13

The 34 are the owner's knowledge of the release plus physical ownership of the disc — "I own the CD, that is 320kb/s mp3 sourced". No detector was involved at any point. This is the project's only owner-attested wild 320 kb/s source.

The 19 are the eye, deliberately and with the reason recorded: CD1+CD2 are the positive class that scores detectors, so a metric-driven relabel of CD3 would have made every future score on the enlarged class self-referential. ⛔ Two limits on that second tier, both of which weaken it. First, five tracks were examined and nineteen were ruled: the panel covered CD3's hardest cases and the ruling was extended to the disc.
